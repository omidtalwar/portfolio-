Who we are

This policy is provided by [Company legal name], located at [registered address], the data controller responsible for your information.

§02

Information we collect

We keep collection to what the ledger actually needs. Daftar does not connect to your bank, and we do not ask for bank login credentials or card numbers — every transaction is one you enter or import yourself.

Information collected automatically

  • Device & app data — device model, operating system, app version, language, and time zone.
  • Usage & diagnostics — which features are used and anonymous crash and performance reports, so we can fix problems and improve the app.
  • Purchase data — your subscription status, plan, and purchase history (managed through the app stores and our subscription provider). We never receive your full payment card details.
§03

  • To provide the core ledger: saving your entries and syncing them across your devices.
  • To generate summaries, categories, and insights from the data you enter.
  • To manage your account, subscription, and access to paid features.
  • To keep the app secure, prevent abuse, and debug crashes.
  • To respond to your support requests.
  • To send essential service messages (for example, about changes to this policy). Marketing messages, if any, are only sent with your consent and can be turned off at any time.

Where the GDPR or similar laws apply, we rely on these bases:

§05

Storage & security

Your ledger data is stored on Google Firebase infrastructure and protected in transit and at rest with industry-standard encryption. Access is restricted to authenticated requests tied to your account.

No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information and to detect and respond to incidents. If a breach affects your data, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

You can exercise most of these directly in the app, or by contacting us at [privacy@yourdomain]. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.
