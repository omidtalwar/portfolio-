Privacy Policy

For the Daftar mobile application (also known as MyDaftar; Android package app.mydaftar).
Effective date: 13 July 2026  ·  Last updated: 13 July 2026

Daftar is a personal income & expense tracker. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, who it is shared with, and the choices and rights you have. Daftar is operated by Omid Talwar ("we", "us", "the developer"). By using the app you agree to this policy.

The short version. Daftar works offline and keeps your ledger on your device. We do not connect to your bank, ask for card numbers, or take payments ourselves. If you scan a receipt, the image is processed by an AI service only to read the amounts and merchant. If you back up or sign in, your data syncs to secure Google servers so you can restore it on a new device. You can export or delete your data at any time.

1. Who we are & scope

This policy applies to the Daftar mobile app on Android and iOS and to its supporting cloud functions. It does not apply to any third-party website or service that the app may link to. For privacy purposes, the data controller is Omid Talwar (contact details in section 14).

2. Information we collect

We collect only what the app needs to work. This includes:

a. Financial records you enter

Transaction amounts, currency, category, dates, and any merchant/source names, notes, tags, and "tax-deductible" flags you choose to add. This is data you create; it is the core of the app. Daftar does not access your bank accounts, and we never collect bank login credentials or full payment-card numbers.

b. Receipt images

If you use receipt scanning, the photos you capture or select are uploaded to secure storage and processed to extract structured data (see section 4).

c. Account information

By default Daftar signs you in anonymously (no personal details required). If you choose to back up your data, we collect the identifier needed to link the account: your email address (email/password sign-in) or a token/identifier from Google or Apple Sign-In. We do not receive your Google or Apple password.

d. Diagnostics & usage data

Aggregated, non-identifying analytics about how features are used (e.g. an entry was saved, a receipt was scanned), and crash/diagnostic reports, to keep the app reliable. Basic device information (device model, OS version, app version, language) may be included in these reports.

e. Purchase status

If you buy a subscription, we receive the purchase/entitlement status (active or not) from the app store and our subscription provider (section 12). We do not receive or store your card details.

3. How we use your information

  • To provide the ledger: record, display, and total your income and expenses.
  • To extract data from receipts you scan (section 4).
  • To back up and sync your data across your devices, and restore it if you get a new device.
  • To authenticate you and secure your account.
  • To operate optional features such as recovery suggestions, reports, and reminders.
  • To maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the app, and to prevent abuse.
  • To process subscriptions and provide support.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your financial records for advertising.

4. Receipt scanning & AI processing

Receipt scanning is optional. When you scan a receipt, the image is sent over an encrypted connection to our secure cloud function, which passes it to a third-party AI provider, Anthropic (the Claude API), solely to read the receipt and return structured fields (such as merchant, total, currency, date, and line items). You review and confirm this data before it is saved.

  • The AI is used only to extract information from the image you provide.
  • The receipt request is transient at the AI provider and is not used to train their models via the business API we use.
  • The original receipt image is stored in your secure cloud storage (section 5) so you can view it later, and is deleted when you delete the associated entry or your account.

Manual and voice entry never send an image to the AI provider.

5. Where your data is stored & sync

Daftar is offline-first: your ledger is stored locally on your device and works fully without a network connection. When you are signed in and online, your data is synced to and stored on Google Firebase / Google Cloud servers so it can be backed up and restored. Receipt images are held in Firebase Cloud Storage. Access is protected by security rules that limit each account to its own data.

7. Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account exists so the app can function. Entries you delete are removed from your live ledger and are permanently purged during routine clean-up. When you delete your account, your synced financial records and receipt images are deleted from our systems. Data stored only on your device is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

8. Your rights & choices

Regardless of where you live, you can:

  • Access & export your data — export is available in the app, and always works even without a subscription.
  • Correct any entry by editing it.
  • Delete individual entries, or your entire account and its data.
  • Withdraw consent for optional features (e.g. stop using receipt scanning).

Depending on your location (for example, under the EU/UK GDPR or California's CCPA/CPRA), you may also have rights to object to or restrict certain processing, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. To exercise any right, use the in-app controls or contact us (section 14).

9. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), access-controlled cloud storage with deny-by-default security rules, and app-integrity verification (App Check) to reduce abuse. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information and review our practices regularly.

10. Children's privacy

Daftar is not directed to children and is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. International data transfers

Our service providers may store and process data on servers located outside your country, including in the United States and the European Union. Where required, transfers are made under appropriate safeguards provided by those providers. By using the app you understand your data may be processed in these locations.

12. Subscriptions & purchases

Some features may require a paid subscription. Purchases are processed by the Google Play Store or Apple App Store and managed through our subscription provider (RevenueCat). We receive only your subscription status, not your payment details. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Google Play or App Store account settings.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, provide a notice in the app. Your continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
